Counter-specimen: a pane that is menu-shaped and dialogue-shaped and is NOT
blocked. The harness is mid-turn; the menu decorates that wait and closes itself.

Placeholders stand in for the surrounding tool output and its paths. The menu
and its own words are unaltered.

  <tool output elided>

  Our systems are thinking a bit more about this request before
  responding.
  Hang tight or retry with a faster model for a quicker response,
  though it may be less capable of handling complex requests.

› 1. Retry with a faster model
  2. Dismiss and keep waiting
  3. Learn more

  No action is required. Codex will keep waiting, and this menu will
  close when the response is ready.

Read as `!occupied! (authority unknown)`, matched by the collar's occupancy rule
`^› [0-9]+\. `. A send to it is queued rather than typed, and queueing is the
right answer: the window completes a turn on its own and the queue drains at it.
